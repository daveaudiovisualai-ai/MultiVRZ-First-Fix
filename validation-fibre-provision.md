# Samsung fibre provision validation

The supplied Samsung crib sheet identifies the AAE Snow Box requirement as an LC–LC link using OM3 or OM4 bend-insensitive multimode fibre (BIMMF) for runs up to 100 m. The project therefore specifies two pre-terminated 75 m OM4 LC–LC BIMMF links. The document is hosted in the web project and linked from the Connection Rack and Consumables views for traceability.

The U3 rack panel now contains eight SpeakOn positions, two five-pin DMX positions and two green LC-labelled fibre feedthrough positions. The two LC duplex D-series feedthroughs terminate at the rear of U3 alongside the DMX connections. The rack parts register and Calculated Materials register each show two 75 m links plus two matching U3 feedthrough adapters.

The implementation includes a compatibility notice: this fibre selection is appropriate only when the final Samsung controller is AAE. CS4B requires its supported LR4 single-mode LC / OS2 or SR4 multimode Type B female MPO design to be selected and confirmed separately.

Mobile screenshots at 390 px confirm that the Rack summary exposes the two 75 m fibre links alongside the scheduled cable counts, while the Consumables page exposes the two-link fibre allocation and the AAE compatibility notice without clipping.
